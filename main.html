A softer side of Fedora

Fedora, tidied up and made warm.

Kuhl is a small customization for Fedora Workstation: warm colors, one consistent font, a Ctrl+Space launcher, and fewer unnecessary extras. No bundled AI features or complicated layers.

01 / The idea

A familiar desktop.
A better feeling.

No big reinvention. Just a few thoughtful changes to make a fresh Fedora Workstation feel a little more like home.

01 / LOOK & FEEL

Look

A warm dark theme (light variant too), matching icons and cursor, one font family, a few wallpapers.

03 / KEEP IT SMALL

Extensions

Four or fewer. Extensions are what breaks when GNOME updates, so the list stays short.

04 / STAY PROTECTED

Security

Nothing is turned off or replaced. SELinux, the firewall and Fedora’s update system stay as shipped.

05 / NO UNNECESSARY EXTRAS

Less bloat. No AI.

No bundled AI assistants, chatbots or background AI services. The planned setup trims optional apps without removing Fedora’s essential tools. Add what you want with Flatpak.

Made to feel familiar

The little things make a place feel yours.

Kuhl is still Fedora underneath. It isn't a new distribution, a replacement update system or a complicated stack of modifications. It stays close to stock Fedora — just cleaner and cozier.

02 / Before you begin

A little room to run.

Kuhl follows Fedora Workstation’s hardware compatibility, with an 8 GB memory baseline for a comfortable everyday desktop.

Memory · Kuhl minimum8 GB RAM

16 GB is a nice upgrade if you keep lots of apps and browser tabs open.

Free storage40 GB

80 GB or more and an SSD are recommended for extra space and speed.

Processor64-bit PC

Compatible Intel or AMD x86_64 hardware, suitable for Fedora Workstation.

Graphics & displayFedora-ready

Graphics capable of running the Fedora GNOME desktop. Hardware support varies.

To install Fedora, also have an internet connection and a 4 GB or larger USB drive for the installer.

8 GB RAM is Kuhl’s chosen minimum, not Fedora’s absolute minimum. Check Fedora’s official requirements ↗

03 / Get settled in

Make yourself at home.

Start from a fresh Fedora Workstation on a compatible PC with at least 8 GB of RAM. When the Kuhl setup script is released, follow its instructions to apply the theme, launcher and lean app defaults.
