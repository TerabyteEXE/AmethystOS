Desktop will not start

At the login screen, choose Fedora GNOME. Inspect your Niri config and check the shell.

niri validate
rpm -q noctalia
amethyst-doctor

Noctalia bar or settings absent

noctalia msg status
noctalia msg settings-open

Check the user-session environment and log out and back into Niri. Noctalia v5 uses noctalia msg, not the old Quickshell command.

Recover an older desktop

amethystos-uninstall --dry-run
amethystos-uninstall

The uninstaller refuses to overwrite modified managed paths. See the installation guide for backups and migration.

Need help?

Gather the exact error message and relevant logs, then open an issue in the project repository.
