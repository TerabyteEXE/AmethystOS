Welcome to AmethystOS

AmethystOS is Fedora Workstation with a carefully adapted copy of Gigi's Rice. Niri manages windows. Noctalia Shell v4 provides the launcher, status bar, quick controls, calendar, notifications, settings, and wallpaper browser as one integrated desktop.

Required version: this original rice needs Noctalia v4.7.7, not the incompatible v5 package in Fedora 44. See installation notes.

Themes and wallpapers

AmethystDark.png and AmethystLight.png are included. Noctalia supports matching light/dark color schemes, wallpaper switching and bar/widget customization. Original anime wallpapers from Gigi's repository were intentionally not copied.
