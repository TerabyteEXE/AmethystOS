Welcome

AmethystOS uses Fedora, Niri and Noctalia v5. The Niri compositor arranges windows; Noctalia provides the bar, launcher, notifications, calendar and settings.

A Welcome to AmethystOS app appears at first login. Open it again from the application launcher any time.

Essentials

Super+SpaceOpen or close launcher
Super+1–5Switch workspaces
Super+Shift+SSettings
Super+WClose active window
Ctrl+Super+KAll keyboard shortcuts
