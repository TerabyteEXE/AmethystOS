Desktop appearance

Use Super+Shift+S for Noctalia settings. Change the bar, widgets, wallpaper, layout and interface scale from its graphical controls.

Dark and light wallpapers

Amethyst Dark and Amethyst Light are included. You can change them in the wallpaper picker or via the amethyst-theme dark and amethyst-theme light commands.

Configuration files

Noctalia v5 reads TOML files from ~/.config/noctalia/; settings applied in the GUI take precedence and are saved in ~/.local/state/noctalia/settings.toml. Niri settings are in ~/.config/niri/.

Do not manually overwrite your managed configuration without saving a backup.
