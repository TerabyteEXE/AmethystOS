Appearance and desktop controls

Use Super + Shift + S to open Noctalia's settings. This is the same shell that draws the top bar, calendar, notifications, launcher and popups. You do not need to configure Waybar or separately themed GTK launchers.

Amethyst Dark and Light

AmethystOS ships a purple color scheme for both variants and copies your original wallpapers to ~/Pictures/wallpapers/. To change themes, use Noctalia Settings or the following commands once the shell is running:

amethyst-theme dark
amethyst-theme light

Existing selections are preserved during Amethyst upgrades; the installer only applies the default wallpaper on the first Noctalia session when no choice is saved.

Fine-tuning the bar and widgets

Use the Noctalia Settings panel. The settings live at ~/.config/noctalia/settings.json. The bar layout, attached panels and plugins originate from Gigi's Rice.

Niri windows, shortcuts and animations

Compositor modules live under ~/.config/niri/modules/. config.kdl loads them. The original Gigi window animation and visual settings are preserved, with Amethyst-purple accents. The hard-coded 1366×768 laptop output was removed, so your monitors are detected normally.

niri validate
niri msg outputs

Back up your own changes before running a config replacement. Existing modified files may be protected or cause the uninstaller to request manual recovery.
